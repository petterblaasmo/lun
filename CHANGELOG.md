# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first Lun.

- A new look for the column, the tabs across the top, Settings and every page, light and dark.
- The address opens where it stands, as one card with its suggestions; a new tab is one larger card.
- Settings is 74 rows on eight pages, each opening on the few most people come for.
- Extensions have a page of their own, and a new extension's button is pinned from the start.
- One motion system: every transition and every press answers the same way.
- Its own name, icon, bundle ID, signing, data folder and keychain items.
- Updates come from Lun's own releases on GitHub.
- No mailing list or iPhone waitlist. Send Feedback opens a draft issue.
