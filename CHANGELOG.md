# Changelog

## Unreleased

- A new version comes as a popup in the window's top corner, once, with Restart and Later. The quiet card in the column stays.

## 0.1.2

- After launch Lun rests in one process, at about 40 MB instead of 66, and the column only draws the rows in sight.
- Pages redraw less for links, forms and address changes.
- File › Export Spaces writes every space to a file, and Bring Things Over brings spaces from Ardia, Yab and Dia.
- Developer Mode for local sites: a bar with the whole address, Web Inspector and Reload Ignoring Cache. Any site can turn it on from its site card or with ⇧⌥⌘I.
- Hide Elements hides everything that looks the same, and can move and resize an element.
- The floating video works with any site's player. Google Meet opens its own mini window.
- A new tab can show your sites as chips on its card. Home bookmarks and your own site styles are on to begin with.
- Pins show a speaker when their page plays sound, and a preview of the page when you hover over them.
- Split View has its controls in a capsule over the page.
- New screenshots appear in Downloads as they land, and a new download flies into the downloads button.
- Paste and Go in the address field's right-click menu. Arrows in Settings put profiles in order.
- Back, Forward and Reload are dimmed rather than hidden when they can't be used.
- Icons only on the bookmarks bar carries over to the sidebar. Return in the address field from another app opens the top suggestion.
- New switches, all off to begin with: Copy captures only, Double click to rename a tab, and the loading line.
- A site that couldn't be found for a moment, as the Wi-Fi changes or a VPN comes up, loads by itself after a few tries.
- Sync with iCloud says why it isn't syncing, tries again, and names the profile that syncs.
- Extensions: NordPass's popup keeps its size, ClickUp Brain starts, and the Fabric clipper no longer reloads pages in a loop.
- Downloads into a folder macOS keeps Lun out of ask once where to save.
- Sign-in windows open after a page notes the step in its address (Teams). SharePoint's and OneDrive's Open in desktop reach Word and Excel.
- ⌘Tab back to a page in full screen brings the full screen back, and screen sharing can pick a video in full screen.
- In Split View a page keeps its caret, and a pin's page moves into the pair as it is.
- A picture inside a link keeps Open Link in New Tab. A typed address that redirects finishes from its own name next time.
- Media keys stay with the tab paused last, What's playing lists every tab playing out of sight, and a music site paused from the sidebar keeps its row.
- Code boxes captioned in other languages are filled, and Apple Passwords rows say which entry they are.
- The hidden sidebar comes out at the screen's edge and leaves a page's own buttons in its band alone; a drag in the page no longer brings it.
- Find a setting answers each key at once. ⌘⌫ deletes the line over an offered address ending. Darken pages keeps frames in their own scheme.
- Deleting a profile before WebKit has started no longer crashes Lun.

## 0.1.1

- Sites load the first time: "Try HTTPS first" is off again, so a site without HTTPS no longer hangs until it times out.
- A link from another app opens in a tab, not in a small window.
- Bookmarks has a page of its own: folders in a tree beside a table or a grid, with drag and drop.
- Passwords can be checked for reused and easy-to-guess ones, on this Mac only, with filters for each.
- Hold a tab over another in the column to split the two. Scroll Together keeps a split's pages in step.
- With the column hidden, resting the pointer at the top edge brings back Back, Forward and the address.
- The floating video shows the live picture straight away, small swipes place it exactly, and a pinch sizes it.
- Tabs opened behind the page load a few at a time, and the session is saved off the main thread.
- A tab is no longer closed by a download, by its page calling close, or by a page handed off from an iPhone.
- A rare crash when a tab closed just after its page drew is fixed.
- The screen-sharing pill can be dragged aside. The column's edge keeps its resize pointer. Add Image to Photos. AppleScript can switch tabs. A short note when a link opens behind a hidden column. A tab's menu says why it stays awake.
- Every control gives under a press and answers the pointer. The last of the Mac's own checkboxes, sliders and pickers are Lun's.
- Settings › Privacy can clear History, Cookies and Cache. Site permissions list the sites you have answered.
- Settings is 72 rows: "Roomier tabs" is gone.
- Signed with Lun's own provisioning profile: a proxy's password and "Always allow" for a coding agent are kept in the Mac's protected keychain again.

## 0.1.0

The first Lun.

- A new look for the column, the tabs across the top, Settings and every page, light and dark.
- The address opens where it stands, as one card with its suggestions; a new tab is one larger card.
- Settings is 74 rows on eight pages, each opening on the few most people come for.
- Extensions have a page of their own, and a new extension's button is pinned from the start.
- One motion system: every transition and every press answers the same way.
- Its own name, icon, bundle ID, signing, data folder and keychain items.
- Updates come from Lun's own releases on GitHub.
- No mailing list or iPhone waitlist. Send Feedback opens a draft issue.
